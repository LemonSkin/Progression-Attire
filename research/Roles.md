# Roles and Armour Sets
## Special Armours
- Adorned items fulfill cold weather "warrior priest" roles. They provide pain shock threshold and cold weather resistance.
- Ashigaru/Samurai/Daimyo armours fulfill a "discplined warrior" role. They provide melee buffs and melee dodge, but are less protective than ordinary European-style metal armours. They take longer to craft due to the expert craftsmanship required to make a light and maneaverable set of armour.
- Leather/Heavy Leather armours fulfill a "Nordic beserker" role. They provide melee buffs and pain shock threshold, but don't provide too much in the way of armour.
- Legionnaire/Centurion armours are unlocked in the neolithic age and can be crafted from wood and metal. They should provide decent protection but since they are unlocked very early in gameplay, they should be outclassed by Medieval tier armours.
- Gilded armours are "prestige". They shouldn't provide any more armour than their non-gilded coutnerparts, but should confer a social impact buff and take an expert craftsman to make. They are truly "show off" items for rich colonies.
- Zweihander armours are for field commanders. They don't necessarily provide any extra armour but give small social impact buffs and are made by more skilled craftsmen.
- Several helmets are going to be made variants instead of separate items.
- SlaveSuppressionOffsets should be added to all armours from chain-tier onwards, though there should be outliers. Adorned, beserker, juggernaut, and zweihander items should have higher values than others in their respective tiers.
