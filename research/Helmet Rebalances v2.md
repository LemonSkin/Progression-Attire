# Rebalance
- Move the decision-making from resources to playstyle.
- Unwind the Medieval Overhaul layering system 
    - DankPyon_InnerHelmet should be removed in favour of OverHead
    - DankPyon_Hood is okay but items should be rebalanced to not provide armour as much as possible
    - DankPyon_Attachment is okay but items should rebalanced or moved to the Shell layer where appropriate.

# Tier 3 Balance (Original → Proposed)
This tier of helmets is broken into three groups to pair with associated roles:
1. Generalist Role: Open helmets: Open Bascinet, Heavy Barbute, Wolf-Ribs Bascinet
2. Vital Specialist Role: Closed helmets: Closed Bascinet, Hounskull Helmet, Klappviser Bascinet, Klappviser Barbute, Sallet (Visored), Sallet (Bellow Face), Sallet (Gryphon)
3. Immovable Object Role: Great helmets: Great Helm, Armet

There are several exceptions to these rules:
- Zweihander (Generalist-tier 0.60 multiplier, paired with Field Commander role for social bonus)
- Heavy Plate Helmet (juggernaut-tier helmet)
- Samurai/Daimyo Helmets (Group 1 armour multiplier, less coverage and make up for with melee buffs, goes with Samurai role)
- Heavy Leather Helmet (Lower than group 1 armour multiplier, make up for with melee buffs, goes with Heavy Beserker role)
